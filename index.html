Placement Wisdom

Quick Resume Improvement Tips

Battle-tested strategies tailored for college students and freshers entering technical and corporate placement drives.

Lead with Powerful Action Verbs

Replace passive expressions like "Worked on" or "Responsible for" with high-impact verbs such as "Engineered", "Architected", "Automated", and "Accelerated".

Example: "Engineered a responsive web dashboard reducing load latency by 35%."

Enforce Rigid Visual Hierarchy

For freshers, keep your resume strictly to one clean page. Maintain consistent 0.5"–0.75" margins, standardized bullet indentations, and clear chronological order.

Rule: Use maximum 2 font families (e.g., Inter/Calibri) and 3 font sizes across headers, titles, and body.

Highlight 2-3 Impactful Projects

Avoid basic tutorial clones (e.g. standard to-do apps). Highlight projects demonstrating end-to-end problem solving, database schemas, API integrations, and edge-case handling.

Checklist: Problem statement → Tech stack → Personal contribution → Measured outcome.

Verify Working Repository Links

Recruiters click your GitHub and portfolio links! Ensure your repositories are public, have an illustrative README with screenshots, and include a live deployment URL where applicable.

Test: Open your exported resume PDF in an incognito browser window and click every hyperlink.

Eliminate Typos & Inconsistencies

Even a single spelling mistake in a technical keyword (e.g. "Javascript", "Pyhton", "Github") signals lack of attention to detail to technical hiring managers.

Pro-Tip: Have two classmates proofread your resume line-by-line before submission.

Tailor Keywords to Job Descriptions

Align your technical skills section with the specific qualifications listed in the company's job notification. Mirror exact industry terms (e.g. RESTful APIs, OOP, Unit Testing).

Advice: Keep a master resume file, then create focused 1-page versions tailored per role type.
